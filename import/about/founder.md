RBANM’s Pre-University College was established by RBANM’s Educational Charities in the year 1971 to cater to the educational needs of the fast growing modern and tech city of Bangalore. This is one of the institutions established under the umbrella of the RBANM’s Educational Charities founded 148 years ago in 1873 by Dharmarathnakara Rai Bahadur Arcot Narrainswamy Mudaliar. Our founder embarked on his vision of social regeneration, providing for all classes and castes in the fields of education, social welfare and religion. Sri Narrainsawmy Mudaliar was born on May 14, 1827 in Arcot, which belonged to the Madras Presidency. Sri Narrainsawmy Mudaliar’s early years were full of struggle and trial. He lost his father at an early age and had shouldered the responsibility of a large family.

He did not have the opportunity to study English language, so he studied and acquired a deep knowledge of ancient sacred Tamil literature which helped in moulding his character.

Soon after his marriage when he shifted to Bengaluru, he started vegetable and salt business. His hard work and perseverance paid off and later he started a furniture shop in the year 1852. Although he led a simple life he was known as a charitable man who always helped the poor. The Delhi Assembly of royals recognized his service to the poor and honored him with a title “Rai Bahadur”.

Sri Narrainsawmy Mudaliar expanded his business and took up construction contracts. He bagged a partnership contract for the construction of Attarakacheri (eighteen offices). Presently the High Court is housed in that building. This was his major achievement and this earned him a large fortune and the sobriquet “Merchant prince of Bengaluru”. His services were duly acknowledged by the Maharaja of Mysore who gifted him 400 acres of land in Bengaluru and Mysore, all of which was later lost in land reform laws.

Though he himself was not educated, he knew the value of education so he started a charitable institution for poor Hindu boys. He started two Thirukulathar institution for the Dalith, one in cantonment and another in Ulsoor way back in 1875.

He also realized the importance of education for girls in those days and started a girl’s institution in 1886 in Ulsoor, named after his wife Smt. Govindammal. He also started a technical institution with surveying, drawing, and other commercial subjects which faded away after engineering colleges came into existence in 1923.

His furniture shop flourished around the same time and earned the honor of Maharaja Sri Chamarajendra Wodeyar’s visit in 1892 and since then the shop was known to people as “Mysore Maharaja Hall”. Sri. Narrainsawmy Mudaliar was honored with the title “Dharmaratnakara‟ in 1894. Sri. Narrainsawmy Mudaliar realized the importance of education and institutioning for the women and the weaker sections of society, at a time when their welfare was never considered significant.

He formed a charitable trust with family members and appointed a Brahmin and some government officials on the board so that it could be managed without “bias”. Now his great grandson Sri. A. A. Sanjeev Narrain as Secretary and Sri. A. M. Sashindar Narrain as Treasurer is looking after the trust.
